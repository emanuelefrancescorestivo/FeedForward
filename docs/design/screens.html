INLÉSA

1 · The first day

Two required screens, then Today. The energy goal starts at "Keep steady" (changed in Profile). No tab bar during onboarding.

2 · Every day: the diary, faster

Today keeps its layout; the week strip on top shows the days you showed up. Logging: repeat a meal, the CROUS lunch, several items at once, or describe it in your own words.

3 · Progress and the week

Counted weekly, from what was logged: food, cooking, variety and money, never weight or eating less.

4 · Circles

Small, private, invite-only. Only what someone posts is seen: cooking and food, never intake, weight or the diary.

5 · Settings, dark theme, French

Notifications all opt-in, at most one a day, none after 21:00. Dark is forest and soil, as now. Every screen exists in French and English.
