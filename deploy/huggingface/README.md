# FeedForward: demo

A food diary that starts from what you need, not from what to cut: log what
you eat in a few taps, ask for meal ideas that fit your goal, your diet and
your budget, and see the evidence behind every suggestion.

**This is a demo.** Tap "Try the demo" for a fresh account of your own, no
sign-up. Please use made-up details: everything is wiped whenever the demo
restarts. Information, not medical advice.

Best on a phone, or in a narrow window. Source code, decisions and licences:
<https://github.com/emanuelefrancescorestivo/FeedForward> (code MIT; food data
ANSES-CIQUAL and Open Food Facts/Open Prices (ODbL); photos from Wikimedia
Commons, each credited in the app under Sources and licences; see
`DATA_LICENSES.md`).
