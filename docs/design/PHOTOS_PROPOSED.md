# Photos proposed for the first set

*October 2026. The photos for decision 30 (`DECISIONS.md`), listed before
anything is downloaded, as agreed with the owner. Source: Wikimedia Commons,
found through its public search API (metadata only). Accepted licences: public
domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA; none is NC or ND.*

- **62 photos for 66 items:** the 52 recipes and 15 everyday foods (four items
  share a photo with a near-identical recipe). **No photo:** bread and dark
  chocolate (no trustworthy match); it keeps the line icon of its family.
- **Licences:** 17 CC0, 2 public domain, 16 CC BY, 27 CC BY-SA. CC BY and
  CC BY-SA need the author's name and the licence next to the photo: the
  recipe sheet's credit line ("Photo: a similar dish · author, licence") does
  that. A resized CC BY-SA photo stays CC BY-SA.
- **Chosen from the title and description, not yet seen.** After download
  each photo is looked at; one that does not show the dish, shows people,
  brands or packaging, is dropped and replaced, and this list is updated.
- **What the download is:** Commons' own 800-pixel-wide versions (about
  100-250 KB each, roughly 10 MB in all), converted to two WebP sizes
  (128 px square and 800 × 600, about 5 MB in all) stored in
  `backend/feedforward/web/photos/` and recorded in `data/photos.json`.
